# Krutrim

## Answers in minutes. Show your work.

Today, supply chain decisions wait for analysis — and the analysis arrives too late to change the outcome. When
a number does arrive, nobody can see where it came from, so it gets second-guessed or ignored.

Krutrim is a working digital twin of a fulfillment center: a live, running model of the operation. It is built
and it runs today. Right now it shows the building. The next stage makes it answer questions about the building.

---

### What changes

| The decision today | With Krutrim |
|---|---|
| *"Do we make the 16:00 carrier cutoff?"* — a meeting next week | Answered in minutes, with the assumptions attached |
| Staffing planned on industry averages | Staffing planned on **your** actual workforce — each picker modelled from your own scan history, not an industry average |
| Policy changes trialled on the live floor, at real risk | Trialled on the twin first, at no risk |
| Automation business cases built on vendor claims | Built on a simulated baseline of your own operation |

*The workforce model is built from your own pick-transaction data — every associate modelled individually,
including how they slow down, batch work and make mistakes. That is why the answer is yours and not a
benchmark's.*

---

### The path

| Stage | What it delivers | State |
|---|---|---|
| **1. See it** | A real twin of the building — real dimensions, real products, real equipment | **Built** |
| **2. Run it** | A simulation of the operation: orders, labour, flow. Answers *what if* | Next |
| **3. Live it** | Connected to the real feed. Answers *what if, starting from right now* | Planned |
| **4. Trust it** | Validated against your real numbers. Then it recommends. Then, where it has earned it, it acts | Planned |

---

### Why it can be trusted

Most AI hands you an answer you have to take on faith. Krutrim hands you the answer **and its reasoning** —
which inputs it used, which assumptions it made, and what would have to change for the answer to flip.

Speed gets you asked. Showing your work gets you trusted.
